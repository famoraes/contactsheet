# Viewing

## Fit and 100% zoom

The viewer normally fits the whole frame. Press **Z** or **double-click** the
image to zoom to **100%**, where one image pixel is one screen pixel, keeping
the clicked point under the pointer. Press Z or double-click again to return to
fit.

While zoomed, **drag** or **scroll** (two fingers on a trackpad) to pan. The
view pans instantly using the preview; the sharp full-resolution version
appears a moment later ("rendering…" shows in the corner meanwhile). The first
zoom on a frame loads its full-resolution scan, which takes a little longer.

The neutral picker works while zoomed; use Z rather than double-click to
zoom, since double-click is a pick. Crop mode always returns to fit.

## Comparing

| Key | View |
|---|---|
| **B** | **Before**: the automatic conversion, without your adjustments and neutral picks |
| **⇧B** | The raw **negative** |
| **X** | **Clipping**: pure white shown red, pure black shown blue |

A badge in the top-left corner shows which view is on.

## Histogram

The histogram at the top of the Adjust tab shows the red, green and blue
distribution of the current conversion. It always reflects your edited
version, also while Before or Clipping is shown.

## Image only

**Tab** hides the toolbar, panel and filmstrip so the image fills the window.
All shortcuts keep working. Tab or Esc brings the panels back.

## The filmstrip

- Thumbnails show each frame's current conversion, crop included; the number
  is the frame's position in the roll.
- Click, Shift-click and ⌘-click to select; **⌘A** selects all frames shown.
- **← / →** (or Enter) step through frames; the toolbar counter jumps to a
  frame number.
- **⌫** removes the selected frames from ContactSheet. Files on disk and their
  saved edits are not touched.
- The bar above the thumbnails [searches and filters](metadata.md#filtering)
  by metadata.
